Advent Of Code 2025

  • Day 1 — Safe Cracking

    Elves discover project management, and we discover JavaScript's quirky modulo behavior while cracking a safe.

  • Day 2 — Repeated Sequences

    Finding numbers with repeated digit patterns. Sometimes brute force is the elegant solution.

    Greedy digit selection from battery banks, and a lesson in not leaving ugly code behind.

    Iteratively removing accessible paper rolls from a warehouse grid. A lesson in not mutating your input.

    Checking ingredient freshness and merging overlapping intervals. The classic scheduling problem in disguise.

  • Day 6 — Trash Compactor

    Cephalopod math homework and the perils of destructive input parsing. Sometimes you have to go back to the drawing board.
